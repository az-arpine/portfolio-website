Who am I?

I’m a designer and a classical violinist/teacher - detail-oriented and curious, a creative problem-solver, critical thinker and collaborative team player.

Originally from Armenia, my musical journey took me to Switzerland, where I earned my MA degree, and eventually to Massachusetts, where I now live. Alongside my background in music and teaching, I’ve expanded into design through a CareerFoundry UX/UI certification, building a new creative path that blends artistry with user-centered problem solving.

As an aspiring designer, I’ve learned to center my projects around the user and their needs. Using tools like Figma, Microsoft Office, and others, I’ve built a strong foundation in user research, wireframing, and designing both native and responsive web applications. I’m continuously exploring new ways to refine my process, experiment with ideas, and create meaningful, user-centered experiences.

On a beautiful day, I love spending time outdoors with my husband and two active children. You can also find me in my backyard — either visualizing my next project or tending to my garden and flower beds.
