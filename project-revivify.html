Revivify

Project Overview

Revivify is a native mobile app designed for vintage clothing enthusiasts who want a faster, more curated way to buy and sell unique fashion pieces. Unlike general resale platforms, Revivify focuses specifically on vintage style, offering a clean, modern interface paired with features tailored to collectors and everyday shoppers alike.

Objective

  • Design the UI for the mobile version of a vintage clothing sales app
  • Come up with 2 to 3 key functionalities and design the UI for the mobile app.

Problem Statement

Many vintage clothing buyers and sellers rely on cluttered platforms that lack focused tools and community features specific to vintage fashion.

Key Challenges:

  • Difficult to filter by clothing type, size, or condition
  • Generic UI doesn't match aesthetic expectations of the vintage-loving audience
  • Uploading items feels cumbersome and inconsistent

Solution

Design a mobile-first experience that:

  • Makes listing items intuitive and fast
  • Emphasizes fashion-focused discovery (by style, color, era, etc.)
  • Builds community trust through reviews and profiles
  • Reflects a vintage-modern brand identity that resonates with fashion-conscious users

User

Users of Revivify are creative individuals who love fashion as a form of self-expression. They shop and sell clothing using mobile apps and values a smooth, stylish digital experience.
