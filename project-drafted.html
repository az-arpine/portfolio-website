Drafted

Project Overview

Drafted is a minimalist note-taking app designed for quick, simple, and distraction-free capture of ideas. Built with speed and clarity in mind, Drafted lets users jot down thoughts, tasks, and reminders in seconds without unnecessary features getting in the way. With a clean interface and intuitive flow, it makes note-taking effortless—whether for brainstorming, daily to-dos, or spontaneous ideas on the go.

Objective

To design a minimalist note-taking app that enables users to quickly capture, organize, and revisit ideas without distractions. The goal was to prioritize speed, simplicity, and ease of use, ensuring that note-taking feels effortless whether for spontaneous thoughts, daily to-dos, or short reminders.

Reflections and Next Steps

Drafted was a fast-paced project completed over one to two days, giving me the chance to practice rapid ideation and execution. The limited time frame pushed me to focus on core functionality and a clean interface, without overcomplicating the design. While it effectively demonstrates how quick note-taking can be simplified, future iterations could explore adding light organizational features such as tags or folders, syncing across devices, and accessibility improvements. These next steps would help transform Drafted from a quick prototype into a more robust, real-world tool
